Team

ADPS was initiated by Jia Huang, Bingsheng Ru, Willem Jiang, and Bo Liang, with support from its expert group, advisors, organizing members, White Paper workshop participants, and engineering case contributors.

Expert group

Haofen Wang (王昊奋)

Tenured Professor and doctoral supervisor at Tongji University, working on knowledge graphs and knowledge-enhanced language models. His public projects include OpenSPG, KAG-Thinker, MemOS, and AI-Ceping.

Haili Zhang (张海立)

Author of LangGraph in Action and LangChain in Action, LangChain Ambassador, and LFAPAC open-source advocate. His work covers agent frameworks, dynamic workflows, and open-source engineering.

Huisheng Yin (尹会生)

Vice President of Geekbang Technology and a Hall of Fame member of the Tencent Cloud Architects Alliance. His work spans software engineering, technical education, and architect communities.

Dong Zhang (张栋)

Expert engineer and architect at Tencent. He has led infrastructure engineering and production deployments supporting tens of millions of hosts, million-scale source-code repositories, petabyte-scale daily traffic, and trillion-scale compute workloads.

Qingfeng Li (李庆丰)

Senior Director at Sina Weibo, responsible for infrastructure, core business platforms, and AI tooling, with a focus on AI application architecture. He is Vice Chair of the CCF TF Multi-Agent Systems Engineering SIG.

Wei Wang (王伟)

Senior Test Engineer in Baidu's Mobile Ecosystem Quality and Engineering Effectiveness Department. His work covers LLM and agent evaluation, software delivery effectiveness, and AI workflows.

Mo Zhou (周默)

Technology Director at JD Group and Chief Algorithm Architect for User Growth. His work spans artificial intelligence, data systems, software engineering, and R&D effectiveness, and he was named among JD Retail's ten most influential experts.

Initiating members

Jia Huang (黄佳)

AI researcher at A*STAR Singapore and author of Manning's Designing AI Agents · Profile PDF.

Bingsheng Ru (茹炳晟)

Industry Research Consultant at Tencent Research Institute. Vice Chair of the CCF TF Multi-Agent Systems Engineering SIG.

Willem Jiang (姜宁)

Long-term contributor to the Apache Software Foundation. Developer of DeerFlow.

Bo Liang (梁博)

Vice President of Shanghai Dongfang Yiteng Technology. Works on financial-services SaaS and production execution agents.

Advisors

Joey Tianyi Zhou (周天异)

Head of the AI Systems and Agents chapter at IAIC, A*STAR, and Professor at Singapore Management University. Co-author of the two-axis framework paper arXiv 2605.13850.

Scott Druck

Programme Director, co-founder of Serpin and ByLevel, and author of Bounded Agency, a reference architecture for AI systems.

Organizing members

Pingping Bai (白萍萍)

ADPS Secretary, responsible for external liaison and event coordination. She organizes industry conferences including QECon, Agentic AICon, and PM Product Leadership.
