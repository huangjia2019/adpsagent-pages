Team

ADPS is maintained by founding members, an advisor, an expert group, and Blue Book contributors. This page records each person's public background and scope of work in the community.

Founding members

Jia Huang (黄佳) Profile PDF

AGENT ARCHITECTURE · PATTERN LANGUAGE · ENGINEERING EDUCATION

AI researcher at A*STAR Singapore and author of Manning's Designing AI Agents. He also wrote the Chinese book Agent 设计模式 and teaches the Geekbang course Claude Code 工程化实战. His work covers responsibility boundaries, execution topologies, validation mechanisms, and reliability in long-running agent tasks.

ADPS work: Co-developed the cognitive-function × execution-topology framework and maintains the pattern catalog, selection method, bilingual white papers, and enterprise case-report program.

Bingsheng Ru (茹炳晟)

SOFTWARE ENGINEERING · AGENT SYSTEMS · R&D PRODUCTIVITY

Industry Research Consultant at Tencent Research Institute, Vice Chair of the CCF TF Multi-Agent Systems Engineering SIG, and Chief Technology Expert at Codewisdom, Fudan University. His work spans software engineering, agent systems, R&D productivity, and AI-assisted development.

ADPS work: Reviews pattern definitions and applicability from the perspectives of software complexity, R&D productivity, organizational coordination, and engineering evaluation.

Willem Jiang (姜宁)

OPEN-SOURCE INFRASTRUCTURE · INTEGRATION · WORKFLOW

Long-time Apache Software Foundation contributor and member of the Apache Camel community. Public materials record his participation in DeerFlow 2.0 activities, and his public profile identifies him as a DeerFlow developer.

ADPS work: Connects the pattern catalog to runnable open-source infrastructure, with emphasis on routing, orchestration, event flow, integration, and community development.

Bo Liang (梁博)

ENTERPRISE SAAS · FINANCIAL SERVICES · PRODUCTION SYSTEMS

Vice President, founding shareholder, and partner at Shanghai Dongfang Yiteng Technology Co., Ltd., responsible for technology and product. Dongfang Yiteng develops SaaS services for banking and the broader financial-services ecosystem. Its Xinxiangtong service matrix has joint operating arrangements with more than twenty banks and serves more than 50,000 enterprise customers.

ADPS work: Supplies deployment evidence from financial services, HR SaaS, payroll, attendance, tax, and disbursement workflows, including state, authorization, and observability issues in the transition from SaaS to execution agents.

Liangding Yuan (袁良锭)

CASE REPORT 03 · MBSE AND INDUSTRIAL SOFTWARE · MODELING AGENT

Founder of Ark (Yage) in Chengdu. He has sixteen years of experience in industrial software and platform development, including workflow, search, collaboration, and knowledge systems. His current work focuses on productizing AI-assisted SysML and MBSE.

Case report: Natural Language to SysML: A Modeling Agent That Delivers One Diagram at a Time. The report documents typed intermediate representations for elements, relationships, and views, together with scope resolution, write-back gates, resumable clarification, and transaction receipts.

Evidence statement: Organization details, scale figures, and implementation outcomes are supplied by the contributors and have not been independently audited by ADPS. Inclusion records an engineering case; it is not product certification or a claim of universal applicability.
