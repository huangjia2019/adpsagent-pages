Founding members

Jia Huang (黄佳) Profile PDF

Blue Book contributors

Blue Book reports record the constraints, failure history, architecture decisions, and transfer limits of a named system or research prototype. Reports identify the contributor and, where applicable, an organization so readers can assess provenance.

Bo Liang (梁博)

CASE REPORT 01 · ENTERPRISE SAAS · EXECUTION AGENT

Vice President of Shanghai Dongfang Yiteng Technology Co., Ltd. and an ADPS founding member.

Case report: Dongfang Yiteng Execution Agent. The report documents task DAGs, a mechanical state plane, unified session state, and recoverable execution in an enterprise SaaS system.

Yuke Xiong (熊钰柯)

CASE REPORT 02 · GEOSPATIAL SYSTEMS · DATA-PUBLICATION AGENT

Technical architect and startup partner at Xuanxu Technology. His work covers GIS products and project delivery, with a current focus on integrating agent capabilities into enterprise systems.

Case report: Xuanxu Technology GIS Data-Publication Agent. The report covers design-time reasoning assets, a deterministic six-stage runtime pipeline, disk-backed facts, external verification probes, and failure records. The linked report is currently available in Chinese.

Liangding Yuan (袁良锭)

RESEARCH REPORT 03 · MBSE / SYSML · MODELING AGENT

Chengdu-based architect documenting a personal technical research project. He has sixteen years of development and architecture experience across workflow, search, collaboration, authorization, and knowledge systems. He now records AI-assisted MBSE and SysML learning, technical reasoning, and local experiments on mbse.ltd. Ark and AI4MBSE are personal research codenames, not incorporated businesses or operating organizations.

Research report: Natural Language to SysML: A One-Diagram Write-Back Study. The report documents typed intermediate representations for elements, relationships, and views, together with scope resolution, write-back gates, resumable clarification, and transaction receipts.

Source note: Identity, organization, and implementation details in these reports are supplied by their contributors. ADPS publishes the material for technical discussion and has not independently evaluated the related products.
