Blue Book contributors

Blue Book reports record the constraints, failure history, architecture decisions, and transfer limits of a named system or research prototype. Reports identify the contributor and, where applicable, an organization so readers can assess provenance.

Vice President of Shanghai Dongfang Yiteng Technology Co., Ltd. and an ADPS founding member.

Case report: Dongfang Yiteng Execution Agent. The report documents task DAGs, a mechanical state plane, unified session state, and recoverable execution in an enterprise SaaS system.

Yuke Xiong (熊钰柯)

Technical architect and startup partner at Xuanxu Technology. His work covers GIS products and project delivery, with a current focus on integrating agent capabilities into enterprise systems.

Case report: Xuanxu Technology GIS Data-Publication Agent. The report covers design-time reasoning assets, a deterministic six-stage runtime pipeline, disk-backed facts, external verification probes, and failure records. The linked report is currently available in Chinese.

Liangding Yuan (袁良锭)

RESEARCH REPORT 03 · MBSE / SYSML · MODELING AGENT

Chengdu-based architect documenting a personal technical research project. He has sixteen years of development and architecture experience across workflow, search, collaboration, authorization, and knowledge systems. He now records AI-assisted MBSE and SysML learning, technical reasoning, and local experiments on mbse.ltd.
